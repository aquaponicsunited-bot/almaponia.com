Data protection

How we process your personal data when you register for the Almaponia launch event.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Almaponia (Aquaponics United). You can reach us at almaponia@aquaponicsunited.com.

What we use it for

  • Managing your registration and your place at the launch event.
  • Contacting you if the date, time or venue changes.
  • Compiling the attendee list for access control.
  • If you expressly authorise it, publishing images of the event for the project's outreach purposes.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you accept the registration checkbox on the form. You may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of the processing already carried out.

What data we collect

Full name, organisation, town, phone number, sector and role, plus any comments you wish to add and, where applicable, the image release. We do not ask for data that is not needed for the purpose described.

How long we keep it

For the duration of the event and, afterwards, for as long as needed to handle any liability arising from its organisation. Authorised images are kept until you withdraw your consent.

Who can see it

We do not share your data with third parties outside the event's organisation. The technology providers we use (web hosting and the forms service) act as data processors and may be located outside the European Union under the safeguards laid down in the GDPR.

Your rights

You may request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, object to or restrict its processing, or ask for portability by writing to almaponia@aquaponicsunited.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
